Rows 1–2 are fixed at (1,0,1) and (0,1,1). When row 3 lands in the plane they span — that is, R33 = R31 + R32 — the three rows become dependent: the row and column spaces collapse to blue/green planes, and red/purple lines grow out of the origin. Drag the canvas to rotate.
