Plane equation:

1x + 1y + 1z + 0 = 0

normal n(1, 1, 1)
|n|1.73
origin to plane −D/|n|0.00
Two things dragging makes obvious:
• Drag A B C: the plane turns, because you're changing the normal vector itself
• Drag D: orientation doesn't move at all, the plane slides along its normal by −D/|n|

Controls:
• Left-click and drag: rotate the 3D view
• Scroll wheel: zoom
• Yellow arrow: normal vector n = (A,B,C)
• Red/blue arrows: two directions in the plane, built from two cross products
