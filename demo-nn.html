Training a network to fit a quadratic curve

61 inputs · 3 hidden units · forward pass gives a 61×3 activation matrix · error propagates back from the output · learning rate takes one step · watch the loss come down.

① Inputs and targets 61 values of x, gold dots are the shape to learn

\[ \{(x_i,y_i^{\star})\}_{i=1}^{61} \]
The inputs are the points along the x-axis; the gold dots are the supervision. The network hasn't entered yet

② Forward pass: a 61×3 activation matrix one row per sample, three columns for three neurons

\[ H=\sigma(xw_1^{\top}+b_1)\in\mathbb{R}^{61\times 3} \]
Shading shifts as training goes. The columns are no longer the hand-picked \(1,x,x^2\) — they're bends the network learned on its own

③ Forward / backward solid lines compute rightward, dashed lines propagate back

\[ \hat y=Hw_2+b_2,\quad \delta_H=\delta_{\hat y}\,w_2^{\top}\odot\sigma'(z) \]
Backward propagation treats the output error as a probe and walks the same path in reverse

④ Learning rate and convergence every step is \(-\eta\nabla L\)

\[ \theta\leftarrow\theta-\eta\,\partial L/\partial\theta,\quad L=\tfrac1{61}\lVert\hat y-y^{\star}\rVert^{2} \]
\(\eta\) too large: the loss jumps around. Too small: it barely moves

⑤ Current fit teal is the network output · gold dots are the target

\[ \text{forward }x\to H\to\hat y \;\longrightarrow\; \text{backward }\delta_{\hat y}\to\delta_H\to\partial w \;\longrightarrow\; \text{update} \]
While playing, watch the texture in ② and the curve in ⑤ change together — that's learning happening
